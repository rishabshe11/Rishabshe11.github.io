Hi, I’m Rishab.

I’m a car photographer who got hooked on cars before I could drive them. Every car has a stance, a line and a story, and my job is to show what you feel when you walk up to yours.

I shoot around Calgary and the foothills at sunrise, sunset and after dark, on empty back roads and quiet industrial lots. You get a relaxed shoot, a clear plan and edited photos that look as good on a phone screen as they do printed on a wall.

Whether it’s a daily driver, a weekend build or a dealership’s new arrival, I treat it like the cover car.

I’m building my name in Calgary’s car scene, one shoot at a time. I want to connect with owners, builders and clubs, and grow with every car I shoot. Bring me your car and let’s make something worth showing off.
